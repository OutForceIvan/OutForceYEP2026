## How to get to Chardonnay by Astoria from Ayala Malls The 30th

Chardonnay by Astoria is about 1.7 km by car or about 950 m on foot from Ayala Malls The 30th (30 Meralco Ave, Ortigas, Pasig City).

**By car (via Meralco Ave and Capt. Henry P. Javier St):**
1. Exit the mall toward Meralco Ave.
2. Turn right onto Meralco Ave (heading north).
3. Make a U-turn at the U-turn slot.
4. Turn left onto Capt. Henry P. Javier St. Chardonnay by Astoria is on your left.

**On foot:** Walk out to Meralco Ave, head south, then turn left onto Capt. Henry P. Javier St and follow it past PhilSports Arena to the venue.
